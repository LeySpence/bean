Pro Brewing Tips

Water Temperature

The ideal brewing temperature is between 92°C and 96°C (197-205°F). Water that is too hot will over-extract, while water that is too cool will under-extract.

Grind Size Matters

Match your grind to your brewing method: coarse for French Press, medium for drip, fine for espresso. A burr grinder ensures consistent particle size.

Coffee-to-Water Ratio

A good starting ratio is 1:16 (1g coffee to 16ml water). Adjust to taste — use more coffee for a stronger cup, less for a lighter brew.
