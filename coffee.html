Quick Brewing Guide

Pour-Over

Use a medium-fine grind. Pour hot water (92-96°C) in slow, circular motions. Total brew time: 3-4 minutes.

French Press

Use a coarse grind. Steep for 4 minutes with water just off the boil. Press slowly and serve immediately.

AeroPress

Use a fine-medium grind. Brew for 1-2 minutes at 80-85°C. Press steadily for a clean, smooth cup.
