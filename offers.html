Frequently Asked Questions

How does the subscription work?

Once you choose a plan, we roast and ship your coffee on the same day each month. You can choose your preferred delivery date during checkout. Each shipment includes freshly roasted beans with a roast date printed on the bag.

Can I skip or cancel a month?

Absolutely. You can skip any month or cancel your subscription at any time from your account dashboard. There are no cancellation fees or long-term commitments.

How is the coffee shipped?

All subscriptions ship via Royal Mail or tracked courier. Basic and Premium plans use standard shipping (2-3 business days), while Family plans use priority shipping (1-2 business days). All shipping is free.

Can I change my plan?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect from your next billing cycle. If you upgrade mid-cycle, we will prorate the difference.

What if I don't like the coffee?

We offer a satisfaction guarantee. If you are not happy with your coffee, contact us within 14 days and we will send a replacement or refund your purchase — no questions asked.
